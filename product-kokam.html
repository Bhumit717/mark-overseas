Kokam

HS Code 12129910 / 12079940
Form Dried Skins (Halves), Flowers
Appearance Deep Purple / Blackish Red
Moisture 12-14% Max
Purity 100% Natural
Shelf Life 12 Months
Origin Konkan Coast (Maharashtra / Goa), India
Packing 10kg / 25kg Bags / Vacuum Packs

Indian Kokam (Garcinia indica) is a unique fruit from the Konkan coast prized for its tangy flavor and cooling properties. We supply dried kokam skins (Amsul) and flowers. Naturally processed without synthetic additives, our kokam is rich in antioxidants and is a key ingredient in traditional health drinks and culinary sauces.
